06 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email press@miezeer.media.

Where will my story appear?

On outlets in the Miezeer network, such as Artist Uncut, Nuntium Magazine, CEO in Her, Mogul StartUp and Bleed the Magazine. We match outlets to your story and audience on the strategy call.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the deliverables in your package, such as the number of articles and outlets. We don't promise specific views, sales or follower counts.

Do I get to approve the writing?

Yes. You review every press release and feature before it's published.

How much does it cost?

Packages start at $497 for a press release, $1,497 for Feature and $3,997 for Headliner. The Always On retainer starts at $1,297 a month. Those are starting prices; your exact quote follows the strategy call.

How long does it take?

As a guide, a press release usually runs about 1–2 weeks after you approve it, Feature takes about 2–4 weeks and Headliner about 4–8 weeks. Exact timing depends on approvals, event dates and outlet schedules, and we'll confirm it on your strategy call.

What is a social media feature on NBK or Artist Uncut?

A post or story set about you on that brand's social media page. NBK is a social media page, so an NBK feature is social posts only, not an article. Each brand is booked separately.

Can you cover my event or red carpet?

Yes, in the LA area. Coverage of one event is included in Headliner and available as an add-on starting at $997. Events outside LA are quoted separately.

How do I get started?

Fill out the intake form. If you'd rather talk first, email us to book a discovery call.
