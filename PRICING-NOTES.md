# Miezeer PR: pricing notes (internal, not linked from the site)

Prepared Nov 19, 2026 after Aaron asked to "create your own packages and pricing." All prices are **starting** prices in USD. Final quotes follow the strategy call.

## Positioning
- **Lower anchor:** the 2023 pr.miezeer.media/promo plans were $340/mo ("Standart") and $400/mo (10 magazine write-ups + 10 social posts). The network is larger now (12 owned outlets plus partners), so those prices are treated as a floor, not a target.
- **Market position:** Miezeer's outlets are niche and urban entertainment, lifestyle and business titles, which fall in the market's "Tier 3 / low Tier 2" bands. Prices sit in or just above the middle of those bands, well under national-outlet guaranteed PR ($997–$5,000 per placement).
- **Psychological pricing:** every price ends in 97.
- **Honesty:** packages guarantee deliverables (number of releases, articles and outlets), never views, sales or followers.
